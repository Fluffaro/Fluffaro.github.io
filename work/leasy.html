Leasy

A rental manager for small landlords. It shows what's been collected this month and what's still pending, keeps every property, unit and tenant in one place, and turns a paid rent into a receipt you can send over Messenger in one tap.

Role
Solo developer: product, design and build
Platform
Responsive web, phone first
Stack

Status
In progress, freshly redesigned

~ $ cat why.md

Rent day without the notebook

Plenty of landlords with a few units still track rent in a notebook or a group chat: who paid, who's late, what the water bill came to, and which receipt went to whom.

Leasy puts that on one screen. The home view answers "how much came in this month?" at a glance, and everything else, from tenants to utility readings, is one tap away on a phone or laid out side by side on a desktop.

~ $ ls ./features

01

This month at a glance

Home greets you with the rent collected so far against what's still pending, an occupancy ring for how many units are leased, and the latest payments with who paid and who hasn't.

  • Collected and pending totals with a progress bar
  • Occupancy, total units and vacancies
  • Quick actions for properties, tenants, readings and reports

02

Every property, unit and tenant

Each property opens to its units, occupancy and monthly rent. Units show the tenant, their number, the due day and a payment history, and can be edited or reassigned in place.

  • Add properties and units, then assign tenants
  • Tenant search and clear empty states

03

A ledger you can tap

Payments lists every rent for the month with collected and outstanding totals. Tap a status circle to mark a payment paid or unpaid, and filter by all, unpaid or paid.

  • Water and electric readings per unit
  • Reports on income across properties

04

Receipts that look like receipts

Every paid rent produces an official receipt with a receipt number, the property, unit and tenant, the amount and how it was paid, ready to print or share over Messenger, Viber or SMS.

05

Phone first, desktop ready

On a phone Leasy uses a bottom navigation bar and forms open as bottom sheets. On a desktop it switches to a sidebar, wider grids and centered dialogs. Light mode is the default, with a dark teal mode that's remembered between visits.

~ $ cat stack.txt

Under the hood

App

React + TypeScript

Built on TanStack Start with TanStack Router for file based routes and TanStack Query for data.

UI

Tailwind + Radix

Accessible primitives styled with Tailwind, in a teal and coral palette with Bricolage Grotesque and DM Sans.

Forms

React Hook Form + Zod

Typed, validated forms that open as sheets on phones and dialogs on desktop.

Deploy

Cloudflare

Set up to run on Cloudflare through its Vite plugin.
