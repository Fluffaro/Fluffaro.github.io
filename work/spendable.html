Spendable

A personal finance app that answers one question every morning: how much can I spend today? It turns budgets, upcoming bills and payday into a single safe-to-spend number, and keeps every wallet, goal and receipt in one place.

~ $ cat why.md

Less bookkeeping, better decisions

Most budgeting apps are good at recording the past and bad at helping with today. Spendable starts from the other end: it looks at what's left in each budget, what bills are due before payday and what you've already spent this week, then gives you one number you can act on.

Everything is saved on the phone first, so it works without a signal, and syncs to the cloud when you sign in. Logging is fast too: snap a receipt and AI turns it into a transaction you just review and save.

~ $ ls ./features

01

Safe to spend, every day

The home screen leads with what you can spend today, worked out from your category budgets, the bills still due this cycle and the days left until payday. Anything you don't spend rolls into savings.

  • Weekly spending bars against your weekly limit
  • Net worth trend and savings goal progress at a glance
  • Upcoming bills right under the number that pays for them

02

Budgets that warn you early

Set a monthly limit per category and watch it fill. The app flags categories that go over, and nudges you when spending tends to spike near the end of the month.

  • On track, watch and over states per category
  • Plain language tips instead of red numbers alone

03

Insights in a sentence

Each month opens with a one line summary, like "You spent ₱8,985 less than last month, mostly Groceries", followed by where the money went and how the month compares with your six month average.

  • Category breakdown with share of total spend
  • Six month history with the average marked

04

Bills, wallets and rules

Recurring bills show what's due this week and whether your wallets cover it, with a marker when a bill costs more than last time. Balances bring bank accounts, e-wallets, cash and investments into one net worth.

  • Bill reminders with price change alerts
  • Smart rules suggested from how you file transactions by hand
  • Every transaction searchable and filterable by wallet

05

Snap a receipt, skip the typing

Take a photo or pick several receipts from the gallery, and a vision model reads each one into a transaction card with the merchant, total, date, category and line items, ready to review and save.

  • Batch scanning with a review step before anything is saved
  • Smart rules apply to scanned receipts too
  • Google Sheets sync and CSV export for your own records

~ $ ls ./themes

Two moods: Blossom and Midnight

The whole app is themed from one set of tokens, so every screen switches between the warm Blossom look and the dark Midnight look in one tap.

Under the hood

App

React Native + Expo

Expo Router tabs and sheets, NativeWind styling, Zustand stores and native charts.

Data

SQLite, offline first

Wallets, transactions, budgets, bills and goals live in a local database with versioned migrations.

Sync

Supabase + Google Sheets

Signed in users sync changes to Supabase, and transactions can mirror to a Google Sheet both ways.

AI

DeepSeek vision

Receipt photos are read into structured transactions with line items, then reviewed before saving.
